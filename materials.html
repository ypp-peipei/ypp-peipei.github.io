Doctoral research · University of Connecticut

Robust deep reinforcement learning
for distribution systems.

My dissertation studies distribution-system operation and control with distributed energy resources through three connected themes: voltage safety, operating flexibility, and economic operation.

The research progresses from multi-agent PV-inverter coordination and topology-aware control to limited-observability learning, PV–BESS coordination, and hierarchical HVAC control. The associated papers document the methods and evaluations.
