A little about me

I am a postdoctoral research associate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Michigan State University, working in the E-RESILIENCY Laboratory. My research focuses on trustworthy AI for power-system operation and control, distributed energy resources, battery energy storage, and grid resilience. I also work on BreakerWise, translating electrical sensing and AI-based diagnostics into practical circuit intelligence.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Connecticut in 2024, advised by Junbo Zhao. Previously, I earned an M.S. from Lehigh University and a B.S. from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities through a 2+2 program with Beijing Jiaotong University. In 2023, I was a summer graduate intern at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory.

Research directions

Explore my research ↗
01

Safe learning for battery coordination ↗

Combining physics-informed surrogate models and reinforcement learning to coordinate distributed batteries under renewable variability.

02

Grid support for emerging large loads ↗

Planning battery converter capacity to support grid frequency response while preserving data-center ride-through.

03

Learning to control DER-rich grids ↗

Robust and hierarchical learning for voltage regulation, PV–battery coordination, peak-demand management, and flexible building loads.

04

Climate resilience and system analytics ↗

Understanding weather-related grid risk and coordinating flexible resources under changing operating conditions.
