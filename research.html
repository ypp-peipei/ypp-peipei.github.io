Ongoing research

Safe learning for battery coordination

This work studies distributed BESS frequency regulation under cloud-induced PV variability. A surrogate-assisted control architecture combines learning-based decisions with analytic droop control, action shielding, and energy-aware coordination across a battery fleet. Evaluation uses dynamic simulations of modified IEEE 14-bus and 39-bus systems in ANDES.

Accepted conference paper

Grid support for emerging large loads

The project examines the interaction between data-center loads, protection constraints, and distributed energy storage. A protection-explicit planning framework allocates BESS converter ratings at facility and grid-side buses, with reference-model verification on an IEEE 39-bus system. The conference paper is listed as accepted in the September 2026 CV.

Doctoral research program

Learning to control DER-rich grids

My doctoral research connects multi-agent coordination, multi-task learning, and surrogate modeling to distribution-system operation. The work addresses topology changes, incomplete observations, and competing operating objectives. Applications span realistic distribution feeders and hierarchical HVAC/DER control evaluated using a microgrid model comprising 413 buildings.

MTRAC AgBio translation project

BreakerWise: from sensing to circuit intelligence

As Product Development Lead and a Venture Fellow, I work on embedded sensing, waveform analytics, prototype development, and the translation of AI-based circuit diagnostics. The project is supported through MSU’s MTRAC AgBio program. Applications include continuity-critical equipment in farms and production facilities, such as pumps, ventilation, and refrigeration.
